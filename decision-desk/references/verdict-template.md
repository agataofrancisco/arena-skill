# Verdict template

Written by the Chair in plain English. No em dashes, no filler, state each point once. Keep the report under about 1,000 words; the round files hold the detail.

Every number below is copied from `score.mjs`, never computed here.

```markdown
# Decision Desk: [decision in a few words]

[date] · Lenses: [list with weights] · Rounds: [n] · Mode: [full | quick | named]

## Verdict: [GO | GO WITH CONDITIONS | RESHAPE | WAIT | DON'T DO IT]

**The call:** [one or two sentences the founder can act on, naming the option]

Option scores: [A] x.x · [B] x.x · [C] x.x · Support for the resolution x.x/10 · Confidence n/10 · Spread n · Vote: [n] endorse, [n] amend, [n] dissent
[Each override the script applied, in one line, or "none"]

## Scorecard

| Lens | Weight | Final stance | [A] | [B] | [C] | Pillar score | Moved? | Their line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash and ruin | 3 | conditional | 1 | 6 | 7 | 8 | gated B to C default | "[their one line for the founder]" |

Math: [one line per option, copied from the script]

## Why the council landed here

[3 to 5 bullets: the arguments that carried the room, each tagged with the lens and the number or fact it rests on]

## Where they fought

[One short block per crux: the question, who was on each side, who moved and why, how it resolved or why it did not]

## Dissent

[The strongest minority view, at full strength, and what would have to be true for it to be right. If every lens agreed, say so and name the least-tested assumption instead.]

## Conditions

[Each checkable: a number, a date, or a test result. Say which must clear before the irreversible step.]

## What to do next

1. This week: [action] (owner)
2. [Next milestone with date]
3. [Decision date]

**Cheapest test:** [the smallest thing that checks the riskiest assumption before committing]
**Kill criteria:** [the signal that means stop, and what happens then]
**Revisit:** [when to bring this back, and with which numbers]

---
[When built lenses were seated: "Some reasoning here is built from public work by named people. It is simulation, not their advice." Otherwise omit.]
```
